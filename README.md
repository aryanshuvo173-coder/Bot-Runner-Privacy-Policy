Bot Runner Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 2026
1. Overview & Commitment
 ColorBot Runner('we', 'us', or 'our') values your privacy. We design our games to be fun, accessible, and privacy-first. We do NOT collect, sell, or harvest personal identifiable information (PII) like your name, phone number, physical address, or contacts.
2. Information We Handle 
* Local Game Data: Your high scores, distance records, coin balances, selected robot skins, and audio preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences). Losing or uninstalling the app may reset locally held unbacked progress.

 * Google Play Billing: 
In-app purchases are processed securely by Google LLC via Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or financial details. Google provides cryptographic purchase tokens solely to verify purchase delivery.

 * Crash & Performance Data: Standard anonymized diagnostic metrics may be collected by Google Play Console to identify app crashes.

 3.* Children's Privacy
  * ColorBot Runner is suitable for general audiences and adheres to the Google Play Families Policy and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
4. Third-Party Services
 * The app interfaces solely with official Google Play Services for in-app billing verification. For Google's privacy practices, refer to policies.google.com/privacy.
5. Developer & Official Store
If you have questions or inquiries regarding this privacy policy, visit our official Google Play Developer Profile ( https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=8420567031001110013 ) or open the Developer link inside the app.
